The Easiest Way to Sign a PDF Document Online

The days of printing a document, signing it with a pen, scanning it back into your computer, and saving it as a new file are over. Whether you need to self-attest an application form for an exam, sign a legal contract, or approve a corporate invoice, ReduceSize PDF Signer allows you to digitally stamp your signature onto any page of a PDF document in just a few seconds.

How to Self-Attest Documents Digitally

Many government portals (like SSC, UPSC, or State Police) and bank KYC processes require you to upload "Self-Attested" documents. This means your signature must be present on your Aadhar Card, PAN Card, or Marksheet PDF. Here is how you can do it digitally:

  1. Take a clean piece of white, unlined paper and sign your name using a black or blue pen.
  2. Take a clear photo of this signature using your smartphone. (For the best results, you can use our Document Scanner to turn the background pure white).
  3. Upload your main document (PDF) to this tool.
  4. Upload your signature photo, select the "Bottom Right" position, and click "Sign Document". The tool will neatly paste your signature onto the PDF!

100% Private & Offline (No Server Uploads)

Your signature is one of your most highly sensitive pieces of personal data. Uploading it to a random third-party server poses a massive risk of identity theft and document forgery. We take your privacy seriously.

ReduceSize operates on a strictly secure Client-Side Processing Architecture. When you upload your PDF and your signature image, the entire merging process occurs locally inside your device's web browser (utilizing your computer's RAM). Your files and your signature are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers. This guarantees absolute offline privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is it safe to upload my signature here?
Yes! As explained above, ReduceSize operates on a strict zero-upload policy. Your PDF and your signature image are merged locally inside your browser. Nothing is ever sent to or stored on our servers.

Can I choose which page to sign?
Absolutely. If you have a 10-page contract and only need to sign the last page, simply type "10" in the Page Number box. You can also select the exact placement (like Bottom Right, Bottom Left, or Top Right) for perfect alignment.

What formats are supported for the signature?
You can upload your signature in standard image formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP. For the most professional look, we recommend using a PNG file with a transparent background.

What if my PDF is password protected?
Our offline signer cannot edit encrypted or locked files. If your PDF requires a password to open, you must first remove the encryption using our free Unlock PDF Tool before you can add a signature to it.
