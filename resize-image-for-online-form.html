Online forms have rigid, outdated legacy systems that require absolute perfection. If your image is 1KB over the limit, or 1 pixel too wide, it will reject it.

The 3 Pillars of Form Rejection (And How We Fix Them)

1. "File Size Too Large" Error

Forms usually ask for files "Under 50KB" or "Between 10KB and 20KB". When you use standard phone cameras, files are over 3000KB (3MB). If you simply compress them blindly, they become blurry and get rejected by manual verification officers.
The Fix: Our algorithm takes your specific KB request, uses binary search compression, and finds the absolute highest visual quality that mathematically fits just beneath your KB limit.

2. "Invalid Dimensions or Aspect Ratio" Error

If a form asks for 3.5cm x 4.5cm, it means they want a Portrait aspect ratio. If you upload a square photo, the portal will either reject it or stretch your face to look alien.
The Fix: The moment you select a preset (like Passport Photo), our cropper automatically locks into the correct aspect ratio. When you drag your face into the box, it is impossible to get the dimensions wrong. Your face stays perfectly proportioned.

3. "Format Not Supported" Error

Did you screenshot your photo or send it via WhatsApp? It might be saved as a `.PNG` or `.WEBP`. Did you take it on an iPhone? It's likely an `.HEIC` file. Government forms hate these formats and only accept Standard `.JPG` or `.JPEG`.
The Fix: Our Anti-Rejection Encoder ignores your original format. When you click Generate, it paints your photo onto a clean white canvas and exports it strictly as a universally standard Baseline JPEG. No transparent backgrounds, no hidden alpha channels—just pure compliance.
