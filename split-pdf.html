Split, Extract, and Delete PDF Pages Online for Free

Working with large PDF documents can be incredibly frustrating. Whether you have downloaded a massive 100-page government exam notification (like SSC, UPSC, or RRB) but only need the 2-page application form, or you scanned a document and accidentally included a blank page, you need a quick way to clean it up. ReduceSize PDF Splitter is a free, visual tool designed to help you extract exactly the pages you need, or delete the ones you don't, within seconds.

Why You Need a Reliable PDF Splitter

Many online portals and corporate email systems have strict file size limits (often under 2MB). Uploading an entire 50-page PDF just to submit one required page is not only inefficient but will likely cause your upload to fail. By splitting your PDF and extracting only the relevant pages, you drastically reduce the file size and make your submissions clean and professional.

Two Intuitive Modes: "Keep" and "Delete"

We designed our interface to match how people actually edit their documents:

  • ✅ Select to KEEP Mode: This mode is perfect for massive documents. By default, no pages are selected. You visually browse the thumbnails and click only the few pages you need. The tool generates a brand new PDF containing strictly those pages.
  • 🗑️ Select to DELETE Mode: Ideal when your document is mostly fine, but you need to remove mistakes (like a blank page or an unwanted cover). You simply click the pages you want to trash, and the tool keeps everything else.

100% Private: Zero Server Uploads

Most online PDF splitters upload your files to their cloud servers. This is a massive privacy risk if you are handling ID cards, Bank Statements, or medical reports. ReduceSize operates on a Client-Side Processing Architecture. When you upload a file, the splitting, page deletion, and PDF generation happen exclusively inside your own web browser. Your data is never transmitted over the internet, ensuring 100% confidentiality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I delete blank pages from a scanned document?
Yes! Use the 'Select to DELETE' mode, identify the blank pages, click on them, and hit Extract. The tool will generate a new PDF with those pages removed.

Is it safe to split my private bank statement here?
Yes, it is 100% safe. Our tool processes all documents locally in your browser. We have no servers to intercept or view your files, making it the safest option available.
