How to Split or Remove Pages from a PDF

Working with large PDF files can be frustrating. Whether you downloaded a 50-page government notification but only need the 2-page application form, or you scanned a document and accidentally included a blank page, you need a quick way to clean it up. ReduceSize PDF Splitter gives you an interactive, visual way to extract exactly what you need.

Two Intuitive Modes

We designed this tool to match how people actually think about editing documents:

  • ✅ Select to KEEP Mode: Perfect for when you have a massive document but only want to extract a few pages. By default, no pages are selected. You just click the 2-3 pages you want to save, and the tool creates a new PDF with only those pages.
  • 🗑️ Select to DELETE Mode: Perfect for when your document is mostly fine, but you want to remove 1 or 2 unwanted pages (like a blank page or a cover page). By default, all pages are kept. You simply click the pages you want to trash.

100% Private & Secure (No Uploading)

Unlike other PDF editors that require you to upload your sensitive files (like Bank Statements, Resumes, or Marksheets) to their cloud servers, our tool runs entirely inside your web browser. The document is rendered locally on your device, and the splitting process happens offline. Your data is never transmitted over the internet, guaranteeing zero risk of data leaks.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Upload PDF: Select your file. The tool will instantly generate visual thumbnails of every page.
  2. Choose Your Mode: Select either "Keep Mode" or "Delete Mode" based on what is easier for your task.
  3. Click Pages: Visually click on the thumbnails to select or deselect pages. You can also use the "Select All" or "Clear All" buttons to speed things up.
  4. Extract: Click the "Extract & Save PDF" button. A new, clean PDF containing only your desired pages will be generated instantly for download.
