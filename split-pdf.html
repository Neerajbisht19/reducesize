Split, Extract, and Delete PDF Pages Online for Free

Working with large PDF documents can be incredibly frustrating. Whether you have downloaded a massive 100-page government exam notification (like SSC, UPSC, or RRB) but only need the 2-page application form, or you scanned a document and accidentally included a blank page, you need a quick way to clean it up. ReduceSize PDF Splitter is a free, visual tool designed to help you extract exactly the pages you need, or delete the ones you don't, within seconds.

Why You Need a Reliable PDF Splitter

Many online portals and corporate email systems have strict file size limits (often under 2MB). Uploading an entire 50-page PDF just to submit one required page is not only inefficient but will likely cause your upload to fail. By splitting your PDF and extracting only the relevant pages, you drastically reduce the file size and make your submissions clean and professional. Our tool replaces the need for heavy, expensive desktop software by offering a lightweight, browser-based solution.

Two Intuitive Modes: "Keep" and "Delete"

We designed our interface to match how people actually edit their documents. We offer two distinct modes:

  • ✅ Select to KEEP Mode: This mode is perfect for massive documents. By default, no pages are selected. You visually browse the thumbnails and click only the 2 or 3 pages you want to save. The tool will generate a brand new PDF containing strictly those pages.
  • 🗑️ Select to DELETE Mode: This mode is ideal when your document is mostly fine, but you need to remove a few mistakes (like a blank page or an unwanted cover). You simply click the specific pages you want to trash, and the tool keeps everything else intact.

100% Private: Zero Data Leak & Offline Processing

When dealing with personal documents such as Bank Statements, ID Cards (Aadhar, PAN), or Medical Records, uploading them to random internet servers is a huge privacy risk. Most online PDF splitters upload your files to their cloud servers to process them.

We do things differently. ReduceSize operates entirely on Client-Side Processing. When you upload a file to our Splitter, the document is rendered locally on your device's memory. The splitting, page deletion, and PDF generation happen directly inside your web browser. Your data is never transmitted over the internet, ensuring total offline security and zero risk of data leaks.

How to Extract or Remove Pages (Step-by-Step)

  1. Upload Safely: Select your PDF file from your phone or PC. Our tool will instantly generate high-speed visual thumbnails for every page.
  2. Choose Your Mode: Toggle between "Keep Mode" (to extract pages) or "Delete Mode" (to remove pages) at the top of the grid.
  3. Make Your Selection: Click on the page thumbnails. You will see a Green Checkmark (✅) or a Red Trash icon (🗑️) based on your selected mode. You can also use "Select All" to speed up bulk actions.
  4. Generate Document: Click the "Extract & Save PDF" button. A clean, optimized PDF will be generated instantly, ready for you to download.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I delete blank pages from a scanned document?
Yes! Simply use the "Select to DELETE" mode, visually identify the blank pages in the thumbnail grid, click on them, and hit Extract. The final PDF will have those blank pages perfectly removed.

Does extracting pages reduce the overall file size?
Absolutely. The final file size is directly proportional to the number of pages you keep. Removing unwanted pages is one of the best ways to reduce file size. If the resulting PDF is still too large for your needs, you can use our PDF Compressor to reduce it to an exact KB limit.

Is it safe to split my private bank statement here?
Yes, it is 100% safe because the processing happens locally on your device without server uploads. Note: If your bank statement is password-protected, our engine will require you to unlock it first. You can easily remove the password using our free Unlock PDF Tool before splitting the pages.

Do I need an active internet connection to split the PDF?
You only need an internet connection to load the website initially. Once the page has loaded, the actual splitting and extraction process happens offline within your browser environment.

Will the extracted pages lose their original quality?
No. Our tool safely copies the exact binary data of the selected pages. The text, images, and formatting will retain 100% of their original high resolution and quality.
