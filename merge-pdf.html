Absolutely. Because our tool uses powerful browser-based processing, your files are never uploaded to any external server. Your private data remains strictly on your device.

We do not impose any server-side upload limits. You can merge as many files as your device's RAM can handle. For most modern smartphones and desktop computers, merging 20 to 50 files simultaneously is completely effortless.

No. Our tool specifically combines the files while retaining 100% of the original quality and formatting. If the final merged PDF is too large for your specific exam portal, you can easily use our main PDF Compressor tool to reduce its size exactly to the required KB limit.

Does this tool work on mobile devices?
Yes! ReduceSize is fully optimized for all mobile browsers across Android and iOS. You can easily select files from your phone's local storage and merge them on the go.
