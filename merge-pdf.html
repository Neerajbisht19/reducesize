How to Merge PDF Files Safely

Whether you are applying for a government job, submitting college assignments, or organizing your personal documents, you are often asked to upload multiple documents (like a PAN card, Aadhar card, and Marksheet) in a single PDF file. ReduceSize PDF Merger allows you to combine these files effortlessly without risking your privacy.

100% Private (No Uploading)

Most online PDF mergers ask you to upload your highly sensitive documents to their cloud servers. This is a massive privacy risk. We built our PDF tools using advanced client-side JavaScript. When you select your files, the merging process happens entirely inside your own browser (offline). We never see, store, or upload your data. It is the safest way to merge documents online.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select Files: Click the upload box and select two or more PDF files from your phone or computer.
  2. Reorder (Important): The order in which files appear in the list is the order they will appear in the final PDF. Use the Up (⬆️) and Down (⬇️) arrow buttons to rearrange them perfectly.
  3. Merge: Click the "Merge PDFs Now" button. Within seconds, a new combined PDF will be generated and ready for download.

What if my PDF is Password Protected?

If any of the PDFs you select are encrypted or locked with a password (like a bank statement or e-Aadhar), they cannot be merged directly. Our tool will automatically detect locked files and alert you. You can use our Unlock PDF tool to remove the password first, and then come back to merge them.

Does it reduce the file size?

No. This tool specifically combines files without losing any quality. The final size will roughly be the sum of all the individual files. If the merged PDF is too large for your exam portal (e.g., above 1MB), you can easily compress it using our main PDF Compressor tool on the homepage.
