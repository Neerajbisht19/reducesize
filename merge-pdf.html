100% Privacy Guarantee: Zero Server Uploads

The biggest risk with conventional online PDF tools is data theft. Most websites require you to upload your highly sensitive, private documents to their remote cloud servers. Once uploaded, you have no control over who might access or store your ID proofs and bank statements.

ReduceSize operates on a revolutionary Client-Side Processing Architecture. Your files never leave your device, and they are never transmitted over the internet. This guarantees absolute privacy, ensuring your sensitive data remains yours.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is it safe to merge bank statements and ID proofs here?
Absolutely. As mentioned, our tool processes all documents locally inside your web browser. We have zero access to your files, making it the safest option available on the internet for handling highly classified data.

Does merging PDFs reduce their overall file size?
No, merging simply joins the files together end-to-end, so the final file size will be approximately the total sum of the individual files. If the resulting merged PDF is too heavy for your exam portal, you can easily shrink it using our free PDF Compressor Tool.

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can combine at once?
We do not impose any artificial paywalls or file limits. You can combine 5, 10, or even 30 PDFs at once. However, for a lag-free experience, we recommend merging up to 40 files in a single batch, depending on your device's RAM capacity.

What if my PDF is password protected?
Our offline engine cannot merge encrypted files. If you attempt to upload a locked document, the tool will alert you. You must first remove the encryption using our dedicated Unlock PDF Tool before merging it.
