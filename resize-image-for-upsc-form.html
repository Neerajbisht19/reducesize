The Ultimate Guide to Live Cropping & Resizing Images for UPSC Forms

If you are applying for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Civil Services (IAS, IPS, IFS) or the NDA exams, you know that their One Time Registration (OTR) and application portals have the strictest image upload guidelines in India. Unlike other exams that only care about the KB size, UPSC requires you to strictly manage the File Size (KB), the Dimensions (Pixels), and increasingly, the text printed on the photograph.

Failing to meet any of these three criteria results in an immediate upload error, or worse, application rejection at a later stage. ReduceSize.online has built this dedicated UPSC image tool to solve all these problems simultaneously.

Live Cropping & Aspect Ratio Management

The most important part of a passport photo is ensuring your face is centered and prominent. Standard resizing tools often stretch your photo or leave ugly white bars. Our new Live Cropping feature lets you upload your raw photo and then visually drag, zoom, and select exactly which part you want to keep. The cropping box is intelligently locked to the aspect ratio derived from your Width and Height inputs (e.g., 350x350 ensures a perfect square crop), so your final image is never distorted.

Official UPSC Photo & Signature Guidelines (2026)

Before you upload, ensure you understand the exact requirements laid out by the commission:

  • File Size limits: Both the photograph and the signature must be between 20 KB and 300 KB.
  • Pixel Dimensions: The width and height must be a minimum of 350 x 350 pixels and a maximum of 1000 x 1000 pixels.
  • Format: Only .JPG or .JPEG files are accepted.
  • The "Name and Date" Rule: Recent UPSC notifications mandate that the candidate's name and the date on which the photograph was taken must be clearly printed at the bottom of the photo. The date must not be older than 10 days from the application start date.

How Our Advanced UPSC Tool Works

Our tool integrates live cropping, text stamping, resizing, and compression directly in your browser:

  1. Upload the raw photo: Select your high-resolution portrait photo.
  2. Crop & Drag: Use the live cropping box that appears. Drag the photo inside the box and use your mouse wheel (or pinch-to-zoom on mobile) to adjust the selection. Your face should fill about 70-80% of the square area.
  3. Set Pixels: We pre-fill the dimensions to 350x350 pixels (safest setting). Change this to 400x400 if desired. The cropper aspect ratio will adjust automatically.
  4. Set KB: We pre-fill the target size to 250KB.
  5. Add Text Automatically: Check the "Print Text" box. Type your name, date, or both. Our script will automatically generate a thin strip at the bottom of your image and stamp the text securely using your chosen professional font.
  6. Download: Click generate. The final image will be precisely cropped to your dimensions, have your details printed, and weigh exactly as requested.
