The Master Fixer for UPSC OTR Form Rejections

Applying for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Civil Services (IAS, IPS, IFS) exams requires absolute precision. The UPSC One Time Registration (OTR) portal is powered by legacy systems that are extremely unforgiving. If your image is 1KB over the size limit or even 1 pixel off in dimensions, the system will discard your application without explanation.

Why UPSC Portals Reject Photos

Our analysis of common rejection errors on UPSC portals shows three recurring issues:

  • File Size Too Large: UPSC portals enforce strict size caps (up to 300KB). If you upload a 3MB phone photo, it will be rejected instantly.
  • Incorrect Aspect Ratio: UPSC mandates a square ratio for photographs. Uploading a rectangular photo will make your face look distorted or stretched.
  • Invalid Image Format: Portal validators often fail if the image is not a standard Baseline JPEG. Our tool ensures your output is 100% compliant.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact pixel dimensions required for UPSC photos?
UPSC mandates that both your photograph and signature must have a minimum resolution of 350 x 350 pixels and a maximum resolution of 1000 x 1000 pixels. The file size must be between 20KB and 300KB.

How do I add my name and date to the UPSC photo?
Our dedicated UPSC tool has an 'Add Name & Date' feature. Simply check the box, enter your name and the date the photo was taken. The tool will automatically generate a strip at the bottom of your photo.
