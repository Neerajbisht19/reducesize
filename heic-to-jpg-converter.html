Why Does Your iPhone Save Photos as HEIC?

Since iOS 11, Apple devices automatically capture photos in the HEIC (High-Efficiency Image Container) format. While this layout is incredible for saving device storage without losing visual quality, it fails completely when you try to share those files with non-Apple systems. Most Windows computers, image editors, and government application portals instantly reject .HEIC extensions.

Our client-side HEIC to JPG converter bridges this compatibility gap perfectly. It decodes Apple's structural layer and outputs standard JPEG files that you can upload anywhere seamlessly.
