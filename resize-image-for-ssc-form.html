The Master Fixer for SSC CGL, CHSL & MTS Form Rejections

Applying for SSC recruitments—like CGL, CHSL, MTS, or GD Constable—is a competitive journey. The last thing you need is a "File Upload Failed" error right when the server is about to close. The SSC application portal is powered by legacy systems that are extremely unforgiving. If your image is 1KB over the size limit or even 1 pixel off in dimensions, the system will discard your application without explanation.

Why SSC Forms Reject Photos

Our analysis of common rejection errors on the SSC portal shows three recurring issues:

  • File Size Too Large: SSC enforces a strict 20KB-50KB cap for photos and 10KB-20KB for signatures. If you upload a 3MB phone photo, it will be rejected instantly.
  • Incorrect Aspect Ratio: SSC requires a 3.5cm x 4.5cm portrait ratio for photos. Uploading a square photo will make your face look distorted or stretched, which is grounds for rejection during manual verification.
  • Invalid Image Format: The SSC portal only accepts .JPG or .JPEG files. If you upload a modern HEIC (iPhone) or PNG file, the upload validator will fail.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact photo size required for SSC CGL and CHSL?
SSC requires photographs between 20KB and 50KB, in JPEG format, with approximate dimensions of 3.5cm x 4.5cm. Both ears should be visible and the background should be light-colored.

What is the signature size limit for SSC forms?
SSC signatures must be between 10KB and 20KB, in JPEG format, with approximate dimensions of 4.0cm x 2.0cm, signed in black or blue ink on white paper.
