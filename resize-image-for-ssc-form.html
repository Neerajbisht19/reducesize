The Master Fixer for Police Constable & SI Form Rejections

Applying for State Police recruitments—like UP Police, Delhi Police Constable, or Bihar Police SI—is a competitive journey. The last thing you need is a "File Upload Failed" error right when the server is about to close. Most state police forms are powered by legacy systems that are extremely unforgiving. If your image is 1KB over the size limit or even 1 pixel off in dimensions, the system will discard your application without explanation.

Why Online Police Forms Reject Photos

Our analysis of common rejection errors on police portals shows three recurring issues:

  • File Size Too Large: Police portals are designed to handle millions of applications. They enforce strict KB caps (e.g., 20KB or 50KB) to keep their database storage costs low. If you upload a 3MB phone photo, it will be rejected instantly.
  • Incorrect Aspect Ratio: If a form asks for 3.5cm x 4.5cm, it requires a portrait ratio. Uploading a square photo will make your face look distorted or stretched, which is grounds for rejection during manual verification.
  • Invalid Image Format: Many state portals only accept .JPG or .JPEG files. If you upload a modern HEIC (iPhone) or PNG file, the upload validator will fail.

How our "Master Form Fixer" Solves This

Unlike generic compressors that just shrink your file, our tool acts as a dedicated Form Fixer:

  1. Smart Cropper: When you select "Photo" or "Signature" from our tabs, we automatically lock the aspect ratio to the standard dimensions used by State Police boards. You cannot make a mistake while cropping!
  2. Anti-Rejection Encoder: No matter what format you upload (.PNG, .WEBP, .HEIC), our tool forces the output to be a compliant, standard Baseline JPEG that every recruitment portal accepts.
  3. Exact KB Targeting: Our algorithm doesn't just "reduce" size; it precisely compresses your image to fit the exact limit (e.g., 48KB) without sacrificing the sharpness of your face or signature strokes.
