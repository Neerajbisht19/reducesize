Official SSC Image Guidelines (2025-2026)

Before you hit the compress button, make sure your raw photograph adheres to these strict visual rules to avoid rejection:

  • No Spectacles & Caps: SSC explicitly rejects photos where the candidate is wearing spectacles (even prescription glasses), caps, or dark glasses.
  • Background: The photo must be taken against a plain, light-colored background (preferably white).
  • Face Visibility: Your face should cover 70-80% of the photograph. Look straight at the camera, ensuring both ears are clearly visible.
  • Photo Age: The photograph should not be more than three months old from the date of publication of the Notice of Examination.

SSC Photo & Signature Specifications

Our tool automatically sets these, but for your reference, here are the technical limits:

  1. Photograph: Allowed file size is 20 KB to 50 KB. The required format is JPEG/JPG. The exact physical dimensions mandated are 3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height), which roughly translates to 413 x 531 pixels digitally.
  2. Signature: Allowed file size is 10 KB to 20 KB. Sign with a black or blue pen on white paper. The required dimensions are 4.0 cm (width) x 2.0 cm (height), translating to roughly 472 x 236 pixels.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the exact photo size required for SSC forms?
SSC requires a passport size photograph of dimensions 3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height). The file size must be strictly between 20 KB and 50 KB.

What are the signature size requirements for SSC?
Your signature should have dimensions around 4.0 cm (width) x 2.0 cm (height) and the file size must be between 10 KB and 20 KB. Make sure to crop close to the ink before compressing.

Why was my SSC form rejected because of the photo?
The most common reasons for SSC photo rejection are: wearing spectacles or caps, unclear or blurred images, both ears not being visible, or the photo being too old or not following the 3.5cm x 4.5cm aspect ratio.

Does this tool convert cm to pixels automatically?
Yes! A 3.5cm x 4.5cm dimension translates roughly to 413x531 pixels. Our SSC tool pre-fills these exact pixels and locks the crop ratio so your image matches the official guidelines perfectly.
