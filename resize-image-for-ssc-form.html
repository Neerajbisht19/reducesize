SSC Image Size Requirements

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) requires candidates to upload images within specific size limits. These include:

  • Passport size photo: 20KB – 50KB
  • Signature: 10KB – 20KB

If your file does not match these requirements, your form may get rejected.

Why Image Upload Gets Rejected?

  • File size exceeds limit
  • Incorrect dimensions
  • Blurry or low-quality image
  • Wrong format (only JPG/JPEG allowed)
