How to Password Protect a PDF Securely

Sending private documents like bank details, ID proofs, or legal contracts via email or WhatsApp can be risky. If the file falls into the wrong hands, anyone can open and read it. Adding a password to your PDF ensures that only the intended recipient can access your information.

100% Private Encryption (No Uploading)

Most online PDF lockers require you to upload your sensitive files to their cloud servers to add a password. This defeats the entire purpose of privacy! We built ReduceSize Protect PDF differently. By utilizing powerful client-side JavaScript, we encrypt your document entirely inside your own browser. The file and the password you type never touch our servers. It is the absolute safest way to lock documents online.

Double Security: The "Flatten" Feature

Our tool doesn't just add a password; it acts as a security flattener. When you lock a PDF through our tool, it renders every page as a high-quality image layer before encrypting it. This means that even after the recipient opens the PDF with the password, they cannot select, copy, or paste the text inside it. It completely protects your signatures and sensitive data from being easily stolen or modified.
