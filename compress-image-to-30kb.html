Compress Image to 30KB Online for Job Applications & Exams

While 20KB and 50KB are common file size limits, many specific portals—such as state-level PSC exams, university admission systems, and corporate job application portals—require your passport photo or signature to be strictly under 30KB. Finding a tool that hits this specific "middle ground" without destroying the quality of your image can be incredibly frustrating.

Our dedicated 30KB image compressor at ReduceSize.online solves this problem instantly. By analyzing your image and adjusting both the pixel density and JPEG quality, our tool guarantees a clear, recognizable photo that safely passes the 30KB gateway.

The Challenge of the 30KB Limit

A 30KB limit is tricky because it sits right between a low-resolution signature and a standard passport photo. If you use a basic percentage-based compressor, you might end up with an image that is 35KB (which will be rejected) or 12KB (which will be too blurry). You need an algorithm that targets the exact number. Our binary-search compression loop runs up to 25 passes in the background to get as close to 29.9KB as technically possible, giving you the best of both worlds: small file size and sharp visuals.

How to Resize your Photo to 30KB

You don't need any technical skills or software like Adobe Photoshop. Just follow these simple steps:

  1. Upload: Drag and drop your image into the box above, or click to browse files from your mobile gallery.
  2. Select Target: Click the "30 KB" quick target button. The tool will automatically fill the input box.
  3. Process: Hit the "Compress & Download" button. The tool processes the image locally in your browser.
  4. Download: Your optimized 30KB file will appear below. Click download and upload it to your form!

Can I Resize Pixels and KB Together?

Yes! Many university forms don't just ask for a 30KB size; they also mandate specific dimensions, such as 200 pixels width by 230 pixels height. Using our "Target Dimensions" feature, you can type in your required width and height, and set the target size to 30KB. The tool will resize the physical dimensions of the image first, and then apply the compression to meet the KB requirement, providing a 100% compliant photo.
