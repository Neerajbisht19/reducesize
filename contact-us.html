❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Support Edition)

Q: Why is my PDF not unlocking even with the correct password?
A: Some banking PDFs use advanced AES-256 encryption. Our tool tries a standard decrypt first, and then falls back to an advanced bypass protocol. If it still fails, the file might be corrupted.

Q: Are my uploaded files safe?
A: Yes. We operate on a strict Zero-Upload policy. We do not have servers to store your files. Everything happens locally on your computer/phone.
