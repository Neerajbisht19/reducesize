Contacting the ReduceSize Team

We pride ourselves on providing entirely free, privacy-first digital utilities for students, professionals, and photographers. Because our tools run 100% in your browser using client-side JavaScript, the majority of issues can be fixed by simply updating your browser or clearing your cache. However, if you run into persistent errors, we want to hear from you.

How to Report a Technical Issue Effectively

To help our engineering team replicate and resolve your bug quickly, please include the following details when you email reducesize.helpdesk@gmail.com:

  • Device Information: Are you using a Smartphone (Android/iOS) or a Desktop PC (Windows/Mac)?
  • Browser Details: Let us know your browser name and version (e.g., Chrome 120, Safari 17).
  • Action Attempted: Which exact tool were you using? (e.g., PDF Password Remover, 20KB Image Compressor, WebP Converter).
  • Error Description: What exactly went wrong? Did the tool freeze, or did the download not start? Attach screenshots if possible.

Frequently Asked Questions (Support Edition)

Q: Why is my PDF not unlocking even with the correct password?
A: Some banking and corporate PDFs utilize advanced AES-256 encryption. Our offline tool attempts a standard decryption first, followed by an advanced bypass protocol. If it still fails, the file structure itself might be corrupted or restricted against browser manipulation.

Q: Are my uploaded files safe when I use your tools?
A: Absolutely. We operate on a strict Zero-Upload policy. We do not own or rent backend servers to process your files. Every single compression, resize, and PDF split happens locally using your device's own memory. Your files are 100% private.

Q: I sent an email but haven't received a reply yet.
A: As a free platform processing thousands of users daily, our small team receives a high volume of emails. Our standard response time is between 24 to 48 business hours. We appreciate your patience!
