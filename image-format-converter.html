Why Convert HEIC to JPG?

Modern Apple devices, including iPhones and iPads, use the High-Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) format by default. While HEIC is excellent for saving storage space without losing image quality, it presents significant compatibility issues. Windows PCs, legacy software, and almost all government, university, or job application portals simply do not support HEIC uploads.

Our HEIC to JPG Online Converter solves this instantly. It decodes the complex Apple image framework and converts your photos into standard JPEG files, which are universally accepted anywhere on the web.

How to Convert Safely

When dealing with personal documents or private photos, uploading them to a third-party server is risky. That's why ReduceSize operates differently. When you select your files, our JavaScript engine parses and converts the HEIC images entirely inside your web browser. Your photos never leave your device, ensuring 100% data privacy.
