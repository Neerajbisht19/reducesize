How to Convert iPhone HEIC to JPG Online

Modern Apple devices (iPhones and iPads) capture photos in the High-Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) format. While this brilliantly saves storage space on your phone, it causes major compatibility issues. Windows PCs natively struggle to open HEIC files, and almost zero government or university application portals accept them.

To fix this, simply click our "HEIC to JPG" tool above. When you select your iPhone photos, our JavaScript decoder parses the complex HEIC data right on your device and instantly converts it to a standard, universally accepted JPEG format. Because there is no server upload, you can convert dozens of personal photos in seconds with total privacy.

Fixing WebP & Transparent PNGs

  • WebP to JPG: WebP is great for website speed, but terrible for local editing. If you downloaded a WebP image, our tool converts it back into a standard, editable JPG or PNG with a single click.
  • Smart PNG to JPG Conversion: A very common issue when converting a logo or transparent PNG into a JPG is that the transparent background turns pitch black, ruining the image. Our converter features a Smart Canvas Fill. When we detect transparency, we automatically inject a clean, solid white background before generating the JPG. It looks flawless every single time.
