iPhone HEIC to JPG Conversions

Apple devices capture photos in High-Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) format. While this saves space on your phone, almost no government portal, university application, or Windows machine supports it. Select our "HEIC to JPG" tool, drop your iPhone photos, and our internal decoder will instantly parse and convert them to standard JPEGs ready for upload.

Smart Background Fix for PNG to JPG

A common problem when converting a logo, signature, or transparent PNG into a JPG is that the transparent areas turn pitch black, ruining the image. Our converter features a Smart Canvas Fill.
