Why Convert JPG to PNG?

The JPG (or JPEG) format uses "lossy" compression. This means every time you open, edit, and save a JPG file, it loses a tiny bit of quality, eventually becoming blurry and pixelated. The PNG (Portable Network Graphics) format, however, is completely "lossless". It preserves every single pixel perfectly.

If you are a graphic designer, photographer, or student preparing to edit an image (like removing its background or adding text overlays), converting your JPG to PNG is the crucial first step to ensure your image remains crisp and professional.
