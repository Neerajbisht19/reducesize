Going to a local photo studio every time you need passport-sized photographs is both expensive and time-consuming. Most studios charge premium rates just to print 8 copies. With ReduceSize Passport Photo Maker, you can take a picture with your smartphone, arrange it perfectly onto a printable sheet, and take the PDF to any local cyber cafe to get it printed for a fraction of the cost.

How to Print Without Losing Quality (The WhatsApp Problem)

A major mistake students make is creating a passport sheet and sending the JPG file to the print shop via WhatsApp. WhatsApp heavily compresses image files to save data. When the shop owner prints that JPG, the photos come out blurry, pixelated, and unusable for official documents.

The Solution: Export as PDF. Our tool allows you to download your final arranged grid as a PDF document. PDFs do not get compressed by WhatsApp or Email. When the shop prints your PDF file, you get 100% sharp, 300-DPI studio-quality photographs.

Adding Name and Date for SSC & NEET Exams

If you are applying for central government exams like Staff Selection Commission (SSC CGL/CHSL) or NTA NEET, the official notification specifically mandates that your uploaded photograph must have your Full Name and the Date of Photograph printed clearly at the bottom. Without this, your application is liable for rejection.

Our tool has a dedicated "Text Strip" feature designed precisely for this rule. Simply check the box, enter your details, and our smart HTML5 Canvas engine will automatically generate a clean, thin white strip at the bottom of your photo with crisp black text, ensuring maximum compliance without covering your face.

How to Use the Passport Photo Maker

  1. Upload a Clear Photo: Ensure you are facing forward against a plain light-colored wall. Avoid selfies if possible; ask someone else to take the photo from 3 feet away.
  2. Crop the Image: Once uploaded, our smart cropper will lock into the exact aspect ratio of a standard passport photo. Drag the corners to frame your face and shoulders correctly.
  3. Select Paper Size: Choose between a standard A4 Sheet (holds around 30 photos) or a 4x6 Inch photo paper (holds 8 photos, ideal for studio glossy printing).
  4. Add Details (If needed): Enable the text strip if you are generating photos for SSC or UPSC.
  5. Generate & Download: Click generate. Download the file as a PDF and take it to your nearest printer!

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard dimensions for an Indian Passport Photo?
The standard physical dimensions are 3.5 cm in width and 4.5 cm in height. Our tool pre-fills these exact measurements, but you can change them if your specific application requires different dimensions.

How many photos fit on an A4 size paper?
For standard 3.5x4.5 cm photos, an A4 sheet can comfortably hold up to 30 photos, complete with dedicated white borders around each image for easy scissor cutting.

Is this tool safe for my personal photos?
Yes. We value your privacy over everything else. The cropping, text generation, and PDF arrangement all happen locally inside your web browser using your device's memory. No photo is ever uploaded to our servers.
