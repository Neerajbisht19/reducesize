How to Print Your Own Passport Photos Cheaply

If you walk into a photo studio to get 8 passport photos, they will likely charge you upwards of ₹100. However, if you give them a ready-made 4x6 inch digital image that already contains your 8 photos, they will treat it as a standard single photo print, which costs just ₹10. ReduceSize.online Passport Maker allows you to create this exact sheet yourself in seconds.

The 300 DPI Print Standard

Unlike our other web-based image compressors that focus on making file sizes as small as possible (KB), this specific tool does the exact opposite. Printing requires a high density of pixels (Dots Per Inch). When you crop your face using this tool, we extract it at maximum resolution and generate your final grid sheet at 100% JPEG Quality. The file size will be large, ensuring that when it hits the printer paper, there is absolutely no blurriness or pixelation.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Take a Good Photo: Stand against a plain white or light-colored wall. Ensure there is good lighting on your face without harsh shadows.
  2. Upload & Crop: Upload the photo here. Our cropping tool is locked to the standard Indian Passport size (3.5 cm x 4.5 cm). Drag your image so your face and shoulders are clearly visible.
  3. Select Print Settings: Decide if you are printing on an A4 sheet using a home printer, or a 4x6 sheet at a photo lab. Then, enter how many copies you need.
  4. Download your Sheet: Click generate. Our smart algorithm automatically arranges your photos mathematically on a pure white canvas, leaving necessary scissor-cutting borders between each photo. Download and print!

Smart Auto-Capping

To prevent printing errors, our tool calculates the physical dimensions of the paper. An A4 sheet can safely fit a maximum of 30 passport photos, while a 4x6 inch sheet can fit 8. If you accidentally type "50" for a 4x6 sheet, our system will warn you and automatically scale it down to the maximum safe limit, so your photos don't get cut off at the edges.
