Resize Passport Photo to 20KB Online for Government Forms

If you are applying for a government job in India, getting your documents verified online is the first major hurdle. Whether it is the Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), or Banking exams (IBPS/SBI), almost every portal requires a passport-sized photograph compressed to exactly 20KB to 50KB.

Modern smartphone cameras capture images in high resolution, often exceeding 5MB per photo. Uploading a 5MB image to these portals will immediately result in a "File size too large" error. That is why we built this dedicated 20KB image compressor. It safely reduces the weight of your photo while mathematically preserving the clarity of your facial features, ensuring your form gets accepted on the first try.

Checklist for a Perfect Exam Photo

Before compressing your image to 20KB, ensure your original photo meets these strict government guidelines to avoid form rejection:

  • Background: Use a solid white or light-grey background. Avoid outdoor scenery or dark backgrounds.
  • Face Coverage: Your face should cover at least 70% to 80% of the photograph.
  • No Accessories: Remove caps, hats, and dark sunglasses. If you wear prescription spectacles, ensure there is no flash reflection (glare) on the lenses.
  • Recency: The photo should not be more than 3 months old. Some exams (like SSC) may require the date of capturing printed at the bottom.

Why do portals demand a 20KB limit?

During a major recruitment drive, a government server might receive over a million applications within a week. If every applicant uploaded a 5MB photo, the servers would crash under the load, and the data storage costs would be massive. A 20KB JPEG file is highly optimized; it is lightweight enough for servers to handle instantly, yet clear enough for an examiner to verify your identity.

How to achieve exactly 20KB without pixelation

  1. Crop the Extra Space: Use your phone's built-in editor to crop out your torso and the extra background. Focus solely on your face and shoulders.
  2. Upload to the Tool: Drop your cropped photo into the upload area above.
  3. Maintain Dimensions: A standard passport photo is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (roughly 132 x 170 pixels). If the portal demands specific pixels, enter them in the Width/Height boxes.
  4. Compress: The 20KB target is pre-selected. Click 'Compress & Download'. Our tool will run multiple passes in the background to give you the highest quality output within the 20KB limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compress a 5MB smartphone photo directly to 20KB?
Yes. Our compressor scales down the physical dimensions slightly while intelligently adjusting the JPEG compression curve, taking a 5MB image down to 20KB securely.

Is my private photograph safe?
Absolutely. We prioritize your privacy. Unlike other apps that upload your face to a cloud database, ReduceSize processes the image entirely within your own web browser. Your photo never leaves your mobile phone or computer.

What if the portal asks for a file between 10KB and 20KB?
It is always best to stay close to the upper limit to maintain maximum clarity. We recommend entering "18" or "19" in our Custom Target Box so your photo is safely under 20KB but retains high quality.
