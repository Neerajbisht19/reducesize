Standard Passport Size Photo Dimensions in Pixels & KB

Published on June 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Whether you are applying for a visa, a university admission, or a government job, the first requirement is always a "Passport Size Photograph". However, while physical photo studios know the exact paper size to print, online portals demand digital dimensions in Pixels and file sizes in KB.

Entering the wrong pixels can stretch or squash your face, leading to immediate rejection of your application. In this guide, we will break down the exact measurements you need and how to achieve them instantly.

1. What is the Standard Passport Size in India?

In India, the universally accepted standard for a physical passport-size photograph is 3.5 cm (Width) x 4.5 cm (Height). But what does that translate to digitally?

Unit of Measurement Width Height
Centimeters (cm) 3.5 cm 4.5 cm
Millimeters (mm) 35 mm 45 mm
Inches 1.37 inches 1.77 inches
Pixels (at 300 DPI) 413 px 531 px

Note: Portals generally accept a pixel ratio of roughly 4:5. So dimensions like 400 x 500 pixels or 413 x 531 pixels are considered ideal for digital passport photos.

2. Common File Size (KB) Limits

Getting the pixels right is only half the battle. Your digital photo also needs to be light enough to be accepted by web servers. Here are the most common file size limits for Indian portals:

  • SSC & Railway Exams: 20 KB to 50 KB.
  • UPSC Exams: 20 KB to 300 KB.
  • IBPS & Banking Exams: 20 KB to 50 KB.
  • Passport Seva (Govt of India): Less than 50 KB.
  • NTA (NEET/JEE): 10 KB to 200 KB.

4. Golden Rules for Digital Passport Photos

Before you upload, ensure your photo meets these standard verification rules:

  • Background: Keep the background plain white or off-white. Avoid busy backgrounds, trees, or dark colors.
  • Face Coverage: Your face should cover about 70% to 80% of the total photo area. Both ears must be clearly visible.
  • Lighting: Ensure there are no harsh shadows across your face or in the background. Flash reflections on glasses are a strict no.
  • Accessories: Remove sunglasses, hats, and caps. Prescription glasses are allowed only if there is no glare on the lenses.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What does DPI mean and why do portals ask for 300 DPI?

DPI stands for "Dots Per Inch". It is a measure of print quality. A 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm photo scanned at 300 DPI results in an image that is 413 x 531 pixels. Most modern digital compression tools automatically handle resolution perfectly for web uploads.

Will changing pixels make my photo look stretched?

If your original photo is a wide rectangle and you force it into a 413x531 vertical format, it will look squeezed. To avoid this, use your phone's built-in crop tool to crop the image into a vertical portrait shape first, and then upload it to our tool to resize the exact pixels.

Conclusion: Understanding standard passport size dimensions is essential for smooth online applications. By using the standard 413x531 pixel measurement and keeping your file size under 50KB, you can ensure your photo is accepted anywhere, every time.
