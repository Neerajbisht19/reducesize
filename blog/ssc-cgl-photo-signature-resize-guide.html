How to Resize Photo & Signature for SSC CGL Online Form Without Losing Quality

Published on June 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Filling out the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) CGL application form can be stressful, especially when you reach the photo and signature upload section. If your image size is even 1KB over the limit, the portal will throw an error. In this comprehensive guide, we will show you exactly how to resize your photo and signature to meet the strict SSC guidelines using our free online tool.

1. Official SSC CGL Photo & Signature Requirements

Before you start compressing, it is crucial to know the exact specifications mandated by the SSC. Uploading blurred or incorrectly sized images is the number one reason applications get rejected.

Photograph Guidelines:

  • File Size: Must be between 20 KB and 50 KB.
  • Format: Only JPEG/JPG format is accepted.
  • Dimensions: Approximately 3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height).
  • Clarity: The photo should be clear, without spectacles or caps, and taken against a light-colored or white background. Both ears should be visible.

Signature Guidelines:

  • File Size: Must be between 10 KB and 20 KB.
  • Format: Only JPEG/JPG format is accepted.
  • Dimensions: Approximately 4.0 cm (width) x 2.0 cm (height).
  • Clarity: Must be signed in black or blue ink on white paper. Blur signatures will lead to application rejection.

3. How to Compress Your Signature to 20KB

Signatures are tricky because they have a very small file size limit (max 20KB). If you compress them too much, the ink lines become pixelated and unreadable.

  1. Crop your signature image so that there is no extra white space around the ink.
  2. Upload the cropped signature to our tool.
  3. Under the Quick Targets section, simply click the 20 KB button.
  4. Hit Process. The algorithm will preserve the contrast of your signature while keeping it under the 20KB limit.

4. Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many candidates make simple errors that cost them a year of preparation. Make sure you don't do these:

  • Taking a photo of a photo: Do not click a picture of an old printed passport photo. It creates glare and reduces quality. Ask a friend to click a fresh photo against a plain wall.
  • Uploading a horizontal signature vertically: Ensure the orientation of your signature is correct. If it's rotated 90 degrees, the SSC portal will accept it, but human verifiers will reject it.
  • Using WhatsApp to transfer images: WhatsApp compresses images automatically but also ruins the resolution. Transfer photos from your phone to PC via email or USB cable to maintain original dimensions before compressing.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What happens if my photo is exactly 50KB?

It is highly risky to have an image at the exact boundary limit. Some browser uploads calculate sizes slightly differently. Always aim for at least 2-3 KB below the maximum limit (e.g., 47KB for a 50KB limit).
