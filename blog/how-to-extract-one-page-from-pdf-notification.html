How to Extract Only 1 Page from a 50-Page PDF Notification

Published: June 2026 • 5 Min Read

If you have ever applied for a government job like SSC, RRB (Railway), UPSC, or state-level exams, you know that the official notifications are massive. A typical recruitment notification PDF can be anywhere from 40 to 100 pages long.

You don't need to read all 100 pages. Most of the time, you only need to print or upload one specific page—for example, the "OBC/SC/ST Caste Certificate Format" found on Page 54, or the "Physical Fitness Declaration Form" on Page 72. Sending a 100-page document to a cyber cafe just to print one page often leads to confusion and wasted money. Similarly, uploading a 50-page PDF to an exam portal will instantly fail because the file size is too large.

The smartest way to handle this is to split the PDF and extract only the single page you need. In this guide, we will show you how to do this for free, right from your mobile browser.
