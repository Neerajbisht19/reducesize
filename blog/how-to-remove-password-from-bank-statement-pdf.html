Common Bank Statement Passwords in India

If you have downloaded your statement but forgot the password format, here is a quick reference for the top Indian banks (always refer to the specific instructions in your bank's email):

  • HDFC Bank: Usually your Customer ID. For some credit card statements, it might be the first 4 letters of your name followed by the last 4 digits of your card.
  • SBI (State Bank of India): Often the last 5 digits of your registered mobile number followed by your Date of Birth in DDMMYY format.
  • ICICI Bank: Typically the first 4 letters of your name (in lower case) followed by your Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  • Axis Bank: The first 4 letters of your name (in UPPER CASE) followed by your Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format.
  • Payslips & e-Aadhar: Generally the first 4 letters of your name in CAPITAL letters followed by your birth year (e.g., AMIT1990).
