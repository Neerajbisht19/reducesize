Complete Guide to Police Recruitment Form Photos

State Police recruitment boards are notorious for rejecting applications due to small errors in photograph and signature uploads. Each state has a different portal (like UPPRPB for UP Police, or CSBC for Bihar Police), and every portal enforces a completely different set of image dimensions and KB sizes.

Using a standard image compressor often leaves you with an image that is either too large (rejected by the portal) or too blurry (rejected during document verification). ReduceSize.online simplifies this by using our unique State-Wise Smart Presets. You simply select the police force you are applying for, and we lock in the required pixel dimensions and maximum KB size automatically.

General Photo Rules for All Police Exams

No matter which state you are applying to, ensure your original photo follows these ground rules before you crop it here:

  • Background Color: The background must be light-colored. A plain white or light grey background is highly recommended. Do not use busy backgrounds or sceneries.
  • Chest Visibility: Unlike standard passport photos that just show the face, many police applications request photos that clearly show the top of your chest and shoulders.
  • No Accessories: Caps, hats, and dark sunglasses are strictly prohibited. If you wear prescription spectacles, ensure there is no flash reflection/glare on the lenses.
  • Signature Ink: Most police boards explicitly mandate that the signature should be done on a blank white paper using a Black ink pen. Blue ink is sometimes rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the required photo size for UP Police Constable?
UP Police usually requires your photograph to be between 20KB and 50KB, and your signature between 5KB and 20KB. Select 'UP Police' in our tool to auto-set these rules.

Why do police application forms reject photos?
State police applications are very strict. The most common rejection reasons are: wearing a cap or dark glasses, invisible ears, having a dark or busy background instead of plain white/grey, or uploading a blurry image under 10KB.

Is Bihar Police photo size different from Delhi Police?
Yes! Bihar Police is much stricter, often asking for photos under 25KB and signatures under 15KB. Delhi Police typically allows up to 50KB for photos. Our tool adjusts the limits automatically based on the state you select.
