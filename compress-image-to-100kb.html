Compress Scanned Documents & High-Quality Photos to 100KB

While strict 10KB or 20KB limits are applied to tiny signatures and passport photos, a 100KB file size limit is the standard requirement for more detailed, text-heavy uploads. If you are uploading a 10th or 12th marksheet, a university degree certificate, a PAN Card, or an Aadhar card scan to an online application portal, the printed text must be perfectly readable. Shrinking a heavy 5MB smartphone scan to exactly 100KB without making the text illegible is exactly what ReduceSize.online excels at.

The Problem with Basic Document Compression

Most basic image compressors shrink files by drastically reducing the physical dimensions or severely crushing the JPEG quality (causing terrible, blocky artifacts). If you apply basic compression to a marksheet, the grades, name, and official serial numbers become a blurry mess, inevitably leading to a rejected application.

Our dedicated 100KB image compressor takes a much smarter approach. It uses a dynamic balancing algorithm that maintains the highest possible resolution while efficiently encoding the visual data. This preserves the sharp contrast of ink against white paper, ensuring your compressed document looks completely authentic and clear to the human eye.

Why Privacy is Critical for 100KB Documents

When you are compressing sensitive identity documents like Passports, Driving Licenses, PAN cards, or educational certificates, data privacy should be your number one concern. Do not upload these files to random cloud servers or unknown mobile apps.

ReduceSize.online is fully client-side. This means our tool uses your device's own processing power (via your web browser's JavaScript engine) to compress the files. Your documents never leave your smartphone or laptop. There are no server uploads, no temporary storage, and zero risk of identity theft.

How to Compress an Image or PDF to 100KB

Achieve the perfect 100KB size quickly and securely without installing any software:

  1. Choose Your Format: If your document is an image (JPG/PNG), stay on the "Image Tools" tab. If it is a scanned document, switch to the "PDF Tools" tab above.
  2. Upload the File: Click the upload area or drag and drop your scanned file.
  3. Confirm Target Size: Click on the "100 KB" quick-target button (it should be pre-selected).
  4. Compress & Save: Click the process button. Your secure, offline compression will finish in under 2 seconds. Click download, and you are ready to upload to your portal.

Ideal Dimensions for 100KB Document Images

Unlike 20KB files which need to be relatively small in physical dimensions, a 100KB file gives you a lot of freedom. You can easily store a document image with dimensions of 800 x 1000 pixels or even 1200 x 1600 pixels within 100KB, provided the background is a plain white sheet of paper. If your portal requires specific pixel dimensions for document uploads, simply use the "Target Dimensions" inputs in our tool alongside the 100KB size limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my scanned marksheet be readable if I compress it to 100KB?
Yes, a 100KB limit offers plenty of data to retain text clarity. Our algorithm specifically prioritizes high-contrast areas (like text on a white background), ensuring your 10th or 12th marksheet remains fully readable to examiners.

Can I compress a PDF document to 100KB here?
Absolutely! Just switch to the '📄 PDF' tab at the top of the tool, upload your scanned PDF file, and set the target to 100KB. The tool will safely shrink your document right inside your browser.

Why do application portals ask for exactly 100KB?
A 100KB limit strikes the perfect balance for official documents. It is large enough to keep text, official stamps, holograms, and small fonts legible, but small enough to prevent massive server storage costs for the government or university.
