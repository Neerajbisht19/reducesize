Compress Signature to 10KB Online for SSC & UPSC Forms

If you are applying for competitive exams like Staff Selection Commission (SSC) CGL, CHSL, Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), or UPSC Civil Services, you already know the struggle of uploading a scanned signature. Government portals have strict guidelines, usually demanding the signature file size to be strictly between 10KB and 20KB. If your file is even 1KB larger, the portal will reject the upload instantly.

Reducing a high-quality smartphone photo to exactly 10KB without it turning into a pixelated, blurry mess is a common challenge for students. That's exactly where ReduceSize.online steps in. Our advanced, browser-based compressor uses a smart binary-search algorithm that adjusts both image quality and pixel dimensions to help you hit your exact 10KB target flawlessly.

Standard Exam Image Size Requirements

Before compressing your image, it is crucial to know the exact dimensions and file size limits of the exam you are applying for. Here is a quick reference table for major Indian exams:

Step-by-Step Guide to Resize Your Signature to 10KB

  1. Crop the Image First: Do not upload a photo of a full A4 sheet with a tiny signature in the middle. Use your phone's gallery to crop the image so only the white space directly around the ink is visible.
  2. Upload to ReduceSize: Tap the upload box above and select your cropped signature image.
  3. Verify the Target: The tool is already pre-set to target exactly 10 KB.
  4. Compress & Download: Click the "Compress & Download" button. Our tool will process the file securely inside your browser in less than a second. Save the final file to your device.

Pro Tips to Avoid Signature Rejection

  • Use Dark Ink: Always sign using a thick black or dark blue pen on plain white paper (avoid ruled notebook paper).
  • Proper Lighting: Take the photo in natural daylight. Avoid shadows falling directly on the signature.
  • Format matters: Most government portals strictly accept .JPG or .JPEG formats. If your phone saves photos in HEIC or PNG, our tool will automatically convert them to JPG during compression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do my signatures get blurry when reduced to 10KB?
Standard compression tools aggressively drop the image resolution, causing blur. Our 10KB compressor intelligently balances dimensions (width/height) and JPEG compression artifacts to keep the ink strokes crisp and readable.

Absolutely. ReduceSize.online runs entirely on client-side JavaScript. This means your sensitive signatures and thumb impressions never leave your phone or computer. There is zero server upload involved.

Can I compress multiple images at once?
Yes! You can select multiple signature or photo files at once. The tool will individually compress each one to 10KB in a single batch process.
