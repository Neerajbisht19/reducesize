How to Convert JPG and PNG to PDF Online

Converting images to a PDF document is a daily requirement for students, professionals, and anyone handling paperwork online. Whether you need to compile photos of your homework, ID cards, or scanned documents, ReduceSize.online provides a blazing-fast tool to convert them into a single PDF.

100% Secure & Private Conversion

Unlike other PDF tools that force you to upload your sensitive ID cards or personal photos to their servers, our Image to PDF tool works completely offline in your browser. Your images never leave your device. The PDF is generated using your device's memory, ensuring maximum privacy and zero risk of data leaks.

How to use the Image to PDF Tool

  1. Select Images: Click on the upload box and select one or multiple images (JPG, PNG, or WebP) from your gallery or computer.
  2. Review Previews: You will instantly see thumbnail previews of all selected images.
  3. Choose Format: By default, the tool fits your images neatly onto standard A4 pages. If you want the PDF pages to perfectly match the size of your images, select "Fit to Image Size" from the dropdown.
  4. Generate & Download: Click the button, and your PDF will be ready to download in seconds!
