The Best Free Document & Signature Scanner Online

Taking a picture of a document with your smartphone is easy, but making it look like a professionally scanned PDF is incredibly hard. Phone cameras naturally capture the shadows of your hands, uneven room lighting, and greyish-yellow tones on white paper. If you upload these raw images to official portals, they look unprofessional and often get rejected.

Our Online Document Scanner was built to replicate the magic of premium mobile scanning apps right inside your web browser. Without installing anything, you can crop your documents perfectly, eliminate dark shadows, and export a clean, high-contrast PDF or JPG file instantly.

How the "Magic Scan" Engine Removes Shadows

Basic image editors just increase the brightness of the photo, which washes out the ink and makes the document unreadable. Our tool is different. When you click "Apply Magic Filter", our engine uses a sophisticated color-dodge blending algorithm. It mathematically calculates where the shadows are and neutralizes them, leaving the background pure white while simultaneously boosting the contrast of the ink and text. The result is a crisp, professional scan.

Perfect for Extracting Signatures

If you need to upload a signature for an SSC, UPSC, or Bank exam, it needs to look like black or blue ink on pure white paper. Here is how to achieve the perfect signature scan:

  1. Upload a regular photo of your signature taken with your phone.
  2. Use the blue crop box to tightly select only the signature area.
  3. Once processed, select the ⚫ B&W Scan mode.
  4. Use the Ink Darkness Slider and move it to the right. Watch as the grey shadows disappear completely, leaving only your pitch-black signature behind!
  5. Download it as a JPG and upload it to your form.

Why Export as PDF?

If you are scanning Aadhar Cards, PAN cards, Marksheets, or resumes, it is highly recommended to use our red "Download as PDF" button. PDFs are the universal standard for document sharing. They maintain their formatting, prevent unauthorized editing of the text, and look highly professional when printed by the receiver.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download an app to scan documents?
No. This tool runs entirely in your mobile or desktop web browser. It offers professional-grade scanning, cropping, and PDF export without taking up any storage space on your device.

Are my highly sensitive documents uploaded to a server?
We respect your privacy completely. The Cropper tool, the Magic Scan algorithms, and the PDF generator all run 100% locally on your device using HTML5 Canvas. Your Aadhar card, PAN card, or signatures are never uploaded to our servers.

Can I compress the scanned document after downloading?
Yes! If the downloaded JPG or PDF is too large for your application form, you can immediately use our dedicated tools (like "Compress to 50KB" or "Compress to 100KB") to reduce the file size.
