Turn Your Phone Photos into Professional Scans

When you take a photo of a document, a handwritten assignment, or a signature on a piece of paper, the result is rarely perfect. The lighting causes dark gray shadows, the text looks dull, and the background is never purely white. If you upload these raw photos to official portals (like UPSC, Banks, or University admissions), they are often rejected for being unreadable.

ReduceSize.online Document Scanner is a 100% free, browser-based alternative to heavy apps like CamScanner. It uses advanced pixel-manipulation algorithms to instantly clean up your mobile photos without adding any annoying watermarks.

Flawless Cropping & Straightening

Unlike basic tools, our cropper is designed specifically for documents. The crop-box is locked in place so you won't accidentally move it while trying to adjust the edges. Additionally, if you took the photo at an angle, you can use our 360° Straighten Slider to perfectly align the document before cropping.

Customizable Magic Scan Intensity

A common issue with document scanners is that they make the image "too white," causing light pen strokes to disappear. We solved this by giving you full control. After applying the 🪄 Magic Color or ⚫ Pure B&W filter, you can slide the Filter Intensity bar left or right. This allows you to find the perfect balance between a bright white background and bold, readable text.

100% Privacy & Security

Because documents often contain sensitive personal information (Aadhar cards, PAN cards, Bank details), we built this tool to run entirely Client-Side. This means your images are processed directly inside your own web browser. Your private documents are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring complete data privacy.
